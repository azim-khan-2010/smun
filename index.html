About SMUN 2026

Virtual International MUN Conference

SpreTech Model United Nations (SMUN) 2026 is a beginner-friendly, 100% FREE, and fully online MUN conference designed to introduce students to diplomacy, public speaking, position paper writing, and global problem-solving.

Committee & Agendas

United Nations Council Simulation

UN Council

United Nations Human Rights Council (UNHRC)

Agenda

Ensuring Equal Access to Healthcare in Developing Countries

Delegates will discuss ways to improve healthcare accessibility, strengthen national healthcare systems, expand access to medicines and vaccines, improve maternal and child healthcare, and promote international cooperation to ensure quality healthcare for all.

UN Council

United Nations Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)

Agenda

Promoting Sustainable Economic Growth Through Youth Entrepreneurship

Addressing unemployment and global economic disparity by creating supportive policy frameworks, financial literacy channels, and unlocking venture capital for youth-driven innovation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers

Is SpreTech MUN 2026 really 100% free?

Yes! There are zero registration or delegate fees. All training, committee sessions, and electronic certificates are provided completely free.

I am a complete beginner. Can I still join?

Absolutely! SMUN 2026 is specifically designed to be beginner-friendly. Our Executive Board will explain all rules of procedure step-by-step.

How will I receive my country matrix and meeting link?

All matrix allotments, background guides, and virtual meeting links (Google Meet/Zoom) will be sent directly to your registered email address before the conference.
